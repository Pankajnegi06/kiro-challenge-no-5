# Garhwal Region Context

## Overview
Garhwal is the **western region** of Uttarakhand, home to the sacred Char Dham pilgrimage and rugged Himalayan terrain. The culture here has influences from Himachali traditions.

---

## Districts
- Dehradun (State Capital)
- Haridwar
- Tehri Garhwal
- Pauri Garhwal
- Uttarkashi
- Chamoli
- Rudraprayag

## Language & Slang

### Greeting: **Semanya** / Dhakuli
Use this as the standard respectful greeting.

### Kinship Terms
| Term | Meaning | Usage |
|------|---------|-------|
| **Bhulla** | Younger Brother | Waiters, shopkeepers, drivers, friends |
| **Bhaiji** | Elder Brother | Respectful address to elders |
| **Didi** | Elder Sister | Women of respect |
| **Bau** | Father | |
| **Ija** | Mother | |

### Filler Word: **Bal**
- Used at the end of assertive sentences
- "The weather is beautiful today, bal."
- "Come, let's have chai, bal."

### Common Phrases

## Must-Visit Places

### Dehradun
- **Robber's Cave (Guchhupani):** River flowing through cave, locals' picnic spot
- **Tapkeshwar Temple:** Ancient Shiva temple in a cave with natural water drops
- **Sahastradhara:** Sulphur springs with therapeutic properties
- **Paltan Bazaar:** Local market for authentic shopping

### Mussoorie & Landour
- **Landour:** Colonial hill station above Mussoorie, peaceful walks
- **Char Dukan:** Four historic shops, famous for Bun Omelette
- **Lal Tibba:** Highest point, sunset views of Himalayan peaks
- **Company Garden:** Families, avoid on weekends
- **Kempty Falls:** TOURIST TRAP - avoid (crowded, commercialized)

### Tehri
- **Tehri Dam:** Asia's highest dam, lake activities
- **Dhanaulti:** Quiet alternative to Mussoorie, eco-parks
- **Surkanda Devi Temple:** Trek with panoramic views

### Rishikesh
- **Triveni Ghat:** Evening aarti, less crowded than Haridwar
- **Neer Garh Waterfall:** 2km trek, natural pool
- **Beatles Ashram (Chaurasi Kutia):** Abandoned ashram with graffiti art
- **Shivpuri:** White water rafting starting point

### Spiritual Sites (Char Dham)
- **Yamunotri:** Source of Yamuna, trek from Janki Chatti
- **Gangotri:** Source of Ganga, sacred temple
- **Kedarnath:** Most challenging, 18km trek or helicopter
- **Badrinath:** Highest of the Char Dham, accessible by road

## Local Cuisine

### Signature Dishes
| Dish | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Kafuli** | Spinach + fenugreek cooked in iron kadhai. STATE FOOD. |
| **Phaanu** | Mixed lentil soup, thick and hearty |
| **Chainsoo** | Roasted black gram dal, earthy smoky flavor |
| **Jhangore ki Kheer** | Barnyard millet dessert, traditional sweet |
| **Arsa** | Deep-fried rice flour sweet, festival special |

### Where to Eat
- **Dehradun:** Kumar Sweets (local mithai), Orchard (Pahadi thali)
- **Mussoorie:** Lovely Omelette Centre, Landour Bakehouse, Char Dukan
- **Rishikesh:** Chotiwala (iconic, touristy but fun), Little Buddha Cafe

## Seasonal Advice

### Best Time
- **March-June:** Pleasant weather, clear skies
- **September-November:** Post-monsoon clarity, festivals

### Monsoon (July-August)
- **DANGER:** Char Dham routes prone to landslides
- Avoid Chamoli, Rudraprayag districts
- Safer: Dehradun, Rishikesh (lower altitude)

### Winter (December-February)
- Mussoorie/Landour: Snowfall possible
- Higher areas (Uttarkashi onwards): Heavy snow, roads may close
